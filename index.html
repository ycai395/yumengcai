About

I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Robotics at the Georgia Institute of Technology, with an expected graduation date of May 2027. Previously, I received my B.E. in Robotics Engineering from South China University of Technology.

My current research focuses on medical and surgical robotics, including image-guided robotic navigation, soft robotic systems, and AI-assisted analysis of robotic surgical workflows. I am also interested in integrating sensing, perception, control, and learning for intelligent robotic systems.

Selected Research Experience

Robotic Surgery Gap Discovery May 2026 – Present

Georgia Institute of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Jun Ueda

  • Developed an AI-assisted analysis pipeline to identify under-addressed functional needs and research gaps in robotic and laparoscopic surgery.

Data-Driven Single-Cell Analysis of GRM5/Grm5-Associated Microglial States Mar. 2026 – Present

Georgia Institute of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Levi Wood

  • Analyzed mouse and human single-cell data to characterize GRM5/Grm5-associated microglial states.
  • Applied Spearman correlation and FDR correction to identify genes associated with Grm5 expression and compare canonical marker programs.

Image-Guided Robotic Guidewire Navigation System Sep. 2025 – Feb. 2026

Georgia Institute of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Jaydev P. Desai

  • Designed and developed a bidirectionally steerable guidewire and its robotic control platform.
  • Developed a C-arm image-based perception pipeline for robust real-time guidewire tracking.
  • Developed an image-guided path-planning framework for guidewire navigation toward predefined targets.

Miniature Hybrid-Actuated Soft Manipulator with FBG-Based Shape Sensing Sep. 2024 – Mar. 2026

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Yitong Zhou

  • Designed a miniature soft continuum robot integrating cable-driven and pneumatic actuation for minimally invasive procedures.
  • Developed an FBG-based shape-sensing pipeline using a data-driven prediction model and two-segment PCC modeling for shape reconstruction.
  • Achieved independent 6-DoF motion in an 11-mm-diameter soft manipulator.

Soft Wearable Assistive Exosuit Jul. 2024 – Sep. 2024

University of Notre Dame | Advisor: Prof. Margaret Coad

  • Investigated soft and bioinspired design, modeling, and control methods for wearable exosuits and prosthetic systems.
  • Led system-level improvements, coordinated experimental evaluation with the health center, and collected data to improve comfort and usability.

Hydrodynamics of Quadrupedal Robotic Paddling Sep. 2023 – Jul. 2024

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Ye Chen

  • Analyzed hydrodynamic forces, power consumption, and flow-field characteristics of single-leg and quadrupedal paddling across multiple gait patterns, including diagonal and lateral-sequence gaits, with varying swing angles, ranges, and phase relationships.
  • Processed large-scale simulation datasets on Linux (Ubuntu) for quadrupedal paddling analysis.
  • Post-processed and visualized CFD results using Tecplot.

Jellyfish-Inspired Amphibious Soft Robot Jun. 2023 – Sep. 2024

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Yitong Zhou

  • Simulated soft actuators of various sizes to guide the design of the jellyfish-like structure and fabrication processes.
  • Integrated sensing, actuation, embedded electronics, and microcontroller-based control for coordinated robot motion.

Stretchable Fiber-Optic Sensor for Large-Strain Sensing Jan. 2023 – Jun. 2024

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Yitong Zhou

  • Fabricated a flexible, stretchable fiber optic sensor optimized for continuous health monitoring.
  • Characterized sensing performance, including strain range, detection limit, and repeatability.
  • Developed a human–machine interaction prototype that translated user motion into interaction signals using the sensor.

sEMG-Based Gesture Recognition Smart Bracelet Jul. 2022 – May 2023

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Longhan Xie

  • Developed signal-processing and recognition methods to address weak wrist sEMG signals, noise interference, and inter-user variability in gesture recognition.
  • Designed the PCB and array-based sEMG bracelet, deployed cloud-based training models, and developed a CNN-based gesture recognition network.
