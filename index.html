Yumeng Cai

M.S. in Robotics, Georgia Institute of Technology

I am a Master's student in Robotics at Georgia Tech. My research interests center on medical and surgical robotics, soft robotic systems, image-guided navigation, sensing, perception, control, and robot learning. I am particularly interested in developing intelligent and miniaturized robotic systems for minimally invasive interventions.

About

I am pursuing an M.S. in Robotics at the Georgia Institute of Technology, with an expected graduation date of May 2027. Previously, I received my B.E. in Robotics Engineering from South China University of Technology.

My work spans robotic mechanism design, image-guided navigation, soft robotics, wearable assistive systems, robot learning, biomedical data analysis, and computational biomechanics.
