About

I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Robotics at the Georgia Institute of Technology, with an expected graduation date of May 2027. Previously, I received my B.E. in Robotics Engineering from South China University of Technology.

I am particularly interested in developing robotic systems for minimally invasive and image-guided medical procedures, with an emphasis on integrating robotic design, sensing, perception, control, and learning.

Image-Guided Robotic Guidewire Navigation System

Georgia Institute of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Jaydev P. Desai
Sep. 2025 – Feb. 2026

Developed a bidirectionally steerable robotic guidewire and its robotic control platform for image-guided minimally invasive procedures.

My work included real-time C-arm image-based guidewire tracking and an image-guided path-planning framework for navigation toward predefined targets.

Miniature Hybrid-Actuated Soft Manipulator with FBG-Based Shape Sensing

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Yitong Zhou
Sep. 2024 – Mar. 2026

Designed an 11-mm-diameter soft continuum manipulator integrating cable-driven and pneumatic actuation for confined medical procedures.

Developed an FBG-based shape-sensing pipeline combining data-driven prediction and two-segment piecewise constant curvature modeling for real-time shape reconstruction.

Robotic Surgery Gap Discovery

Georgia Institute of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Jun Ueda
May 2026 – Present

Developed an AI-assisted analysis pipeline to identify under-addressed functional needs and research gaps in robotic and laparoscopic surgery.

The project focuses on systematically connecting clinical needs, existing robotic capabilities, and opportunities for future surgical robotic systems.

Other Research Projects

Soft Wearable Assistive Exosuit

University of Notre Dame | Prof. Margaret Coad
Jul. 2024 – Sep. 2024

Investigated soft and bioinspired design, modeling, and control methods for wearable assistive systems. Led system-level improvements and experimental evaluation focused on comfort and usability.

Manta Ray-Inspired Soft Robotic Swimmer

South China University of Technology | Prof. Yitong Zhou
Jun. 2023 – Sep. 2024

Designed a bistable soft swimmer using precurved composites and McKibben muscles for snap-through flapping. Evaluated forward, reverse, turning, and flipping motions.

Hydrodynamics of Quadrupedal Robotic Paddling

South China University of Technology | Prof. Ye Chen
Sep. 2023 – Jul. 2024

Analyzed hydrodynamic forces, power consumption, and flow fields across multiple robotic paddling gait patterns. Processed simulation datasets on Ubuntu and visualized CFD results using Tecplot.

Stretchable Fiber-Optic Sensor for Large-Strain Sensing

South China University of Technology | Prof. Yitong Zhou
Jan. 2023 – Jun. 2024

Fabricated a flexible stretchable fiber-optic sensor and characterized its strain range, detection limit, and repeatability. Developed a human–machine interaction prototype based on the sensor.

sEMG-Based Gesture Recognition Smart Bracelet

South China University of Technology | Prof. Longhan Xie
Jul. 2022 – May 2023

Developed signal-processing and recognition methods for weak wrist sEMG signals. Designed the PCB and array-based sEMG bracelet and developed a CNN-based gesture recognition system.

Data-Driven Single-Cell Analysis of GRM5/Grm5-Associated Microglial States

Georgia Institute of Technology | Prof. Levi Wood
Mar. 2026 – Present

Analyzed mouse and human single-cell transcriptomic data to characterize GRM5/Grm5-associated microglial states. Applied Spearman correlation and FDR correction to identify associated genes and compare canonical marker programs.
