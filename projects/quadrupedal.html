Hydrodynamics of Quadrupedal Robotic Paddling

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Ye Chen
Sep. 2023 – Jul. 2024

Overview

This project investigated the hydrodynamics of robotic paddling through computational fluid dynamics.

Hydrodynamic Analysis

I analyzed hydrodynamic forces, power consumption, and flow-field characteristics across multiple paddling gait patterns.

Simulation and Visualization

I processed large-scale simulation datasets in Linux (Ubuntu) and post-processed CFD results using Tecplot.
