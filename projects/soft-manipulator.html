Miniature Hybrid-Actuated Soft Manipulator with FBG-Based Shape Sensing

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Yitong Zhou
Sep. 2024 – Mar. 2026
Soft manipulator system
FBG-based shape reconstruction

Overview

This project focuses on the development of a miniature soft continuum manipulator for confined medical procedures. The robot integrates cable-driven and pneumatic actuation within an 11-mm-diameter structure.

Hybrid Actuation

I designed the manipulator to combine cable-driven and pneumatic actuation, enabling multiple independent degrees of freedom within a compact form factor.

The system achieved independent 6-DoF motion while maintaining the small diameter required for confined environments.

FBG Shape Sensing

I developed an FBG-based shape-sensing pipeline for estimating the manipulator configuration. The pipeline combines data-driven prediction with a two-segment piecewise constant curvature model for shape reconstruction.

Publication

Yumeng Cai†, L. Gan†, Y. Zhang, X. Wang, and Y. Zhou, “A Miniature Hybrid-Actuated Soft Manipulator with FBG-Based Shape Sensing for Confined Medical Procedures,” submitted to IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Biomimetics, 2026.
