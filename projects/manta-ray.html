Manta Ray-Inspired Soft Robotic Swimmer

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Yitong Zhou
Jun. 2023 – Sep. 2024

Overview

This project explored a manta ray-inspired soft robotic swimmer based on bistable structural mechanics.

Bistable Flapping Mechanism

I designed a bistable soft swimmer using precurved composite structures and McKibben muscles to generate snap-through flapping motion.

Swimming Performance

I evaluated the swimming behavior of the robot and demonstrated multiple motion modes, including forward swimming, reverse swimming, turning, and flipping.
