sEMG-Based Gesture Recognition Smart Bracelet

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Longhan Xie
Jul. 2022 – May 2023

Overview

This project developed a wearable sEMG system for recognizing hand gestures from weak wrist muscle signals.

Signal Processing

I developed signal-processing and recognition methods to address weak wrist sEMG signals, noise interference, and inter-user variability.

Hardware and Learning

I designed the PCB and array-based sEMG bracelet, deployed cloud-based training models, and developed a CNN-based gesture recognition network.
