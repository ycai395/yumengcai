Overview

This work proposes a residual reinforcement learning (RRL) framework for soft robotic arm control that uses an inverse kinematic model as prior knowledge. Instead of learning a complete controller from scratch, reinforcement learning learns corrective pressure residuals around the inverse-kinematic solution.

Soft Robotic Arm and Kinematic Prior

The simulated soft arm consists of two modular three-chamber pneumatic soft actuators. A piecewise constant curvature model and a Jacobian-based inverse kinematic solver provide an interpretable but imperfect prior control solution.

Residual Reinforcement Learning

The policy receives the current tip position, target error, chamber pressures, and payload, then outputs residual pressure adjustments. PPO is used to optimize the residual policy while the inverse kinematic model initializes each episode near a plausible control solution.

Trajectory Tracking

RRL and conventional RL both track circular and Viviani trajectories under payloads from 0 g to 300 g. The principal advantage of RRL is learning efficiency: inverse-kinematic prior knowledge reduces the exploration burden while retaining high tracking accuracy.
