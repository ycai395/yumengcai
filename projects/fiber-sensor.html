Stretchable Fiber-Optic Sensor for Large-Strain Sensing

South China University of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Yitong Zhou
Jan. 2023 – Jun. 2024

Overview

This project focused on the development of a flexible and stretchable fiber-optic sensor for large-strain sensing.

Sensor Development

I fabricated the flexible fiber-optic sensor and characterized key sensing properties, including strain range, detection limit, and repeatability.

Human–Machine Interaction

I developed a human–machine interaction prototype that converted user motion measured by the sensor into interaction signals.
