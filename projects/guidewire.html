Image-Guided Robotic Guidewire Navigation System

Georgia Institute of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Jaydev P. Desai
Sep. 2025 – Feb. 2026
Robotic guidewire system
Image-based guidewire tracking

Overview

This project investigated an image-guided robotic guidewire navigation system for minimally invasive procedures. The system integrates a bidirectionally steerable guidewire, robotic actuation, image-based perception, and navigation planning.

Robotic Guidewire System

I designed and developed a bidirectionally steerable guidewire together with its robotic control platform. The system was developed to provide controllable distal steering while maintaining the slender geometry required for minimally invasive navigation.

Image-Based Tracking

I developed a C-arm image-based perception pipeline for robust real-time guidewire tracking. The image-processing framework extracts the guidewire from fluoroscopic imagery and provides visual information for downstream navigation.

Navigation

I developed an image-guided path-planning framework that uses image-space information to support guidewire navigation toward predefined targets.
