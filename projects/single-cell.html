Data-Driven Single-Cell Analysis of GRM5/Grm5-Associated Microglial States

Georgia Institute of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Levi Wood
Mar. 2026 – Present

Overview

This project applies data-driven analysis to mouse and human single-cell transcriptomic datasets to characterize GRM5/Grm5-associated microglial states.

Data Analysis

I analyzed mouse and human single-cell datasets to investigate associations between GRM5/Grm5 expression and microglial states.

Statistical Analysis

I applied Spearman correlation and false discovery rate correction to identify genes associated with Grm5 expression and compare canonical marker programs.
