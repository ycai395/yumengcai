Robotic Surgery Gap Discovery

Georgia Institute of Technology | Advisor: Prof. Jun Ueda
May 2026 – Present

Overview

This project investigates how AI-assisted analysis can be used to identify under-addressed functional needs and research gaps in robotic and laparoscopic surgery.

Approach

I developed an AI-assisted analysis pipeline for organizing surgical tasks, existing robotic capabilities, and unmet functional needs.

The goal is to systematically connect clinical needs with opportunities for future robotic system development.

Research Direction

The project supports the discovery of potential research opportunities in robotic and laparoscopic surgery by identifying areas where current robotic systems may not fully address clinical requirements.
