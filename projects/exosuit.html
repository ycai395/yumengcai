Soft Wearable Assistive Exosuit

University of Notre Dame | Advisor: Prof. Margaret McGuinness
Jul. 2024 – Sep. 2024
Soft sleeve prototype
Exosuit experimental setup

Overview

This project focused on developing soft wearable assistive systems using fabric pneumatic artificial muscles (fPAMs) for exosuit and prosthetic applications.

System Development

Fabricated soft fabric pneumatic artificial muscles (fPAMs) for wearable exosuit and prosthetic applications, and improved the overall wearable system to make it more comfortable, convenient, and practical for users.
