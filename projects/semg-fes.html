Portable sEMG-Controlled Hand Electrical Stimulation System

Published in IEEE Sensors Journal
2025
Portable CCFES system architecture
Task-oriented object-taking demonstration

Overview

This work developed a portable contralateral controlled functional electrical stimulation (CCFES) system based on bilateral surface electromyography. The system combines gesture recognition, affected-side sEMG feedback, and adaptive FES amplitude modulation.

System Design

The portable system contains a gesture-recognition module, an sEMG-acquisition module, and an FES module connected through BLE. Four hand movements - hand opening, grasping, wrist extension, and wrist flexion - are recognized from the healthy side and used to switch rehabilitation actions.

Adaptive Stimulation

Stimulation amplitude is adjusted using the energy difference between homologous bilateral sEMG signals, enabling affected-side muscle status to enter the control loop rather than relying only on the healthy limb.
